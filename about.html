About the platform

Grounded Academic Research for Impact

Connecting theoretical human geography and empirical urban politics with real-world governance, environmental education, and community dignity.

People and Places was established by Emmanuel Awohouedji, PhD, as an independent urban and environmental research, writing, and consulting platform. Operating with paper-tone restraint, rigor, and quiet intellect, the platform addresses critical urban and environmental challenges across diverse African territories.

By translating academic studies into policy documentation, urban plans, and public-facing short series, People and Places bridges the gap between scholarly inquiry and actionable social impact. By working across different disciplines and partnering with different experts, People and Places provides depth and analysis of urban environmental challenges for policy purposes and actionable social impact.

Human Geography

Analyzing lived experiences, spatial rights, floating habitats, and urban communities across changing landscapes.

Urban Politics & Governance

Evaluating legal texts, policy frameworks, governmental structures, and public space equity.

Environmental Change

Examining climate adaptation, waste management, infrastructure impact, and environmental education.
